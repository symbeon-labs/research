# CASE-004 Baseline v0.1

## Conventional derivation
The conventional condition starts from explicit problem distinctions and derives requirements, then architecture.

## PDI derivation
The PDI condition first constructs a normalized computational representation, then derives capabilities, transformations, and architecture.

Neither condition is allowed to inspect the other's intermediate artifacts.

## Predefined expectation
If PDI's explicit intermediate representation contributes reproducibility beyond conventional derivation, the PDI pair should show higher agreement on capability and architecture sets while preserving coverage.
