# ART-001 — Problem-Derived Intelligence

**Version:** v1.0 FINAL  
**Status:** Research Framework  
**Author:** JX-SH1W4  
**Organization:** SYMBEON — Intelligence Systems Lab

## Abstract

ART-001 introduces Problem-Derived Intelligence (PDI) as a research hypothesis: whether the computable structure of a problem can guide the derivation of the intelligence architecture required to solve it.

The framework starts from problem formalization, infers capability requirements, and derives candidate compositions of models, specialists, tools, evidence requirements and governance mechanisms.

The hypothesis is explicitly open to empirical validation or falsification.

## Core direction

`PROBLEM → FORMALIZATION → CAPABILITIES → ARCHITECTURE → RESOURCES → SOLUTION`

## Research status

This article defines the framework and experimental program. It does not constitute an empirical validation of PDI.

## Next step

The program moves from conceptual framing to controlled experimentation, benchmark design and reproducible evaluation.
