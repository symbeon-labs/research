# SYMBEON Research Protocol

## 1. Research object

Each research claim must be represented as a hypothesis or research question that can be examined through evidence.

## 2. Minimum record

Research work should preserve:

- question or hypothesis;
- representation/version used;
- method or experimental protocol;
- observed evidence;
- result;
- limitation;
- unresolved questions;
- next test.

## 3. Epistemic status

Each important statement should be distinguishable as one of:

- hypothesis;
- observed behavior;
- interpretation;
- design decision;
- unresolved question;
- validated result.

## 4. Falsification discipline

A research line remains open to negative results. Rejection, weakening, boundary conditions and failure modes must remain traceable.

## 5. Reproducibility

Experiments should record enough versioned information to reconstruct what was tested, with which representation, architecture and evaluation criteria.

## 6. Relationship to implementations

Implementations and applications may serve as experimental instruments or evidence sources. They do not automatically validate a research hypothesis.

The research record should remain conceptually independent from any single implementation, product or deployment.
